# Sourcing research log

**Research and verification date:** November 25, 2026 (Ottawa). Page ages and availability varied. No accounts, purchases, outreach, quotes, credentials or expenses.

## Search passes

1. **Discovery:** English and French Canadian thrift, auctions, liquidation, screen-printer/distributor closeouts, charity channels, graders and pre-processing textiles; early market comparison for the United States, Europe and East Asia.
2. **Native-language:** Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, Malay, Thai, French and Spanish keywords and local marketplace/category paths. German and Dutch supplier pages inspected; no claim of a comprehensive native-language search in every market. Regional grading contacts discovered through India, Pakistan and Malaysia operators.
3. **Competitor and directory:** Followed catalogue links, seller pages, product grades and policies rather than leaving the work at results-page snippets. Used a second available web search connector as a coverage check; the tools' underlying search-engine independence is not verifiable. Opened later category pages and deeper directory sections as well as the first results.
4. **Verification:** Cross-checked product pages, known price and width/length fields, bid vs fixed vs sold status, destination coverage, shipping policies, import law and original terms. Captures with missing or 403 product pages stay indexed-only; no access-control bypass.
5. **Synthesis:** Scored reviewed sources using documented observations, kept discovery-only names unscored, generated repeatable CAD sensitivities and drafted test-buy gates.

## Exclusions and holds

- **GoodwillFinds** not treated as a current storefront: [trade coverage reports shutdown](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/goodwill-shutters-online-marketplace-goodwillfinds/151013/). ShopGoodwill is separate and active. Other closed/uncorroborated lookalike domains excluded.
- **Vintage premium, bulk-only and mystery boxes:** not excluded from discovery, but evaluated separately. Sui & Shara's examples were well over target. KOOP Rescue, C-grade bales and auctions are not guaranteed fits. Historical Bulk Vintage and VOX price lists were not treated as live prices.
- **No demonstrated channel:** Poshmark, eBay, and similar general platforms treated as baselines, not the main answer. No evidence that ordinary Toronto screen printers allow sale of customer-overrun stock; contact-only, seek rights. Recycled hospital/industrial textiles are not assumed wearable. Government insignia, customer IP and counterfeits are excluded from a buying pilot without proper rights.
- **Direct access failures:** Salvation Army product detail returned 403 through research tools; some Asian and Canadian URLs returned cache miss, stale captures, or 404. Those examples retain source/availability flags. Numerous robots URLs could not be fetched. No inference of permission, no forced alternative access.
- **Lookalikes:** ItsADeal and ThatsADeal have different businesses/sites. Raghouse and Vintage Rag House appear on distinct first-party surfaces but corporate ownership unverified; the latter is screen-only. Do not count a duplicate wholesale listing as independent available supply. Brand routes such as ZOZOUSED within ZOZOTOWN and 2nd Street regional chains were not counted as multiple businesses merely for geography.

## Open verification questions

- Ottawa residential and commercial postcode quotes for Raghouse, Thrift2U, Black Cat Ko., Quebec thrift and prototype Ontario carton. No carrier or proxy transaction was tested.
- Current price, defect distribution, actual images and 25/50-piece availability for shortlisted graders. Certification, legal entity, payee and actual stock should match before paying.
- Whether high-supply retailers can provide authorized public feeds or one-time CSV files; raw timestamps and explicit photo/data reuse rights are usually not public. No permission tested.
- Many examples are category snapshots or sold records. No exhaustive daily availability census or stock history. No claim of three live items with both dimensions for every supplier. Some listed price/returns terms require member/checkout views or a real quote.
- Regional coverage is uneven: strong Canada/US/Japan samples; Korea/Taiwan/Hong Kong/Thailand/Malaysia/India/Pakistan reconnaissance; not a representative survey of these industries. Markets depending on LINE/Instagram/TikTok/WhatsApp were considered through verified supplier links, not private communications or accounts. Small India/Pakistan shipments and European forwarding remain conditional.
- Price model is a screening estimate; lots and order thresholds may not be divisible. There is no observed personal fit yield until the user authorizes a trial.

# Sourcing secondhand T-shirts for Ottawa

Research date: **November 25, 2026**. Prices below are CAD unless marked otherwise. No orders, accounts, quotes, messages or expenses were initiated.

## Executive summary

Three different markets answer three different needs. **Canadian markdowns and Ottawa stock** are the best place to test under $8 without an import bill. **Raghouse and domestic graders** offer the best unit economics on uncurated lots, but only if the usable fraction holds up. **Japan** is the best depth-for-measurements market, not the cheapest ordinary-tee market after per-listing fees. A one-seller lot changes those economics substantially.

The best immediately evidenced bulk offer was [Raghouse's unsorted 71-piece graphic/sports lot](https://raghouse.com/collections/t-shirts/products/graphic-sports-t-shirts-unsorted-71-pcs), **US$68** (about $97 merchandise). The variant was shown available and listed 13.154 kg shipping weight. A $95 freight plus $15 broker-fee scenario works out to **$4.22/usable at 75%**, or **$6.33 at 50%**. A $180 postal stress case is **$6.13** and **$9.20**, respectively. Neither freight figure is a quote; the [seller states it actually uses UPS to Canada](https://raghouse.com/pages/faq), so compare its checkout against the stress benchmark, not a promise of a postal option.

The best local price benchmark is [Lainey's Luck](https://laineysluck.ca/info/), which lists **$4.99/lb** for its Ottawa bins (about **$2.42 per 220 g tee** before tax, in person). [Black Cat Ko.'s $1 dollar bin](https://blackcatko.ca/collections/dollar-bin) was mostly **not** target tees, but its [men's catalogue](https://blackcatko.ca/collections/mens) showed actual graphic tees at $4–$4.50; current stock is unconfirmed. For fit data, [ItsADeal](https://itsadeal.ca/collections/mens-tops) gave measured chest and length on three product checks. Its [Ontario shipment policy](https://itsadeal.ca/policies/shipping-policy) makes the **$65 free-shipping threshold** more important than single-item price: **$22** below it. [People's Thrift](https://www.peoplesthrift.ca/shipping-policy) offers **$9.99 Ontario** combined shipping, with a [19.5″ width / 25.5″ length](https://www.peoplesthrift.ca/product-page/pop-man-men-s-graphic-print-t-shirt-m) graphic tee observed at $10.40 in a newer index and $10.14 in an older capture; neither is an assured checkout price.

## 2. Ten unconventional leads

1. **[Bank & Vogue](https://www.bankvogue.com/our-products/graded-goods/), Ottawa office:** ask for a local grader referral or segregated jersey cartons, rather than buying a container of mixed rags.
2. **[Canadian Textile Recycling](https://www.canadiantextilerecycling.com/Recycling.html):** buy upstream before packing if they agree. Its published mixed rags average around 1,000 lb; ask for a splitter, not a bale.
3. **[Bassi Trade](https://www.bassitrade.com/), Brampton:** try an intact tee pre-export grade and request a real sample with varying weights and graphics.
4. **[Evergrow Trade](https://evergrowtrade.com/), Toronto:** find the intact cotton stream before industrial wiper cutting.
5. **[Five Star Rags](https://www.fivestarrags.com/products), Toronto:** a small carton of low-grade/uncurated jersey is the inquiry, not a confirmed offer.
6. **[Manji Trading](https://vintaget-shirt.com/), North York:** its 20/30/50-piece themed bundling is documented; ask whether a less curated corporate / event pull exists.
7. **[Stitchco](https://www.stitchco.ca/) and neighboring print shops:** request authorized samples, cancelled jobs or off-spec print runs. No printed surplus sale was verified; ask about logo permissions and seconds.
8. **[GCSurplus](https://gcsurplus.ca/) and [Hometown Auctions](https://hometownauctions.ca/):** Ottawa/Gatineau pickup search for defunct-event stock, team tees, or mixed estate clothing. An Ottawa scrap-textile lot is evidence of a category, not proof of wearable stock.
9. **[Warada Trading Plus](https://waradatrading.com/), Thailand:** Thai-language 23-tee subset at roughly ฿2,300 or larger black-tee bale at ฿4,500 advertised; exact grade and Canadian export quote missing.
10. **[Lotus Recyclers](https://lotusrecycle.com/), Kandla SEZ:** explore export-ready intact cotton before wiper manufacturing. Try [Flax](https://flaxapparels.com/used-clothing/) and [Maruti](https://marutiexportskandla.com/) for competing samples, not a speculative import deposit.

## 4. Landed costs and consolidation

All values below are **CAD per usable item at an assumed 75% yield**, rounded. The CSV also has 25%, 50%, 90% and 1/5/10/25/50/100-unit models. Unknown/under-minimum sizes are not purchase recommendations.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Ottawa bins at $4.99/lb | 3.65 | 3.65 | 3.65 | Estimated 220 g/tee, in-person handpick, excludes travel and time. |
| Domestic $1 markdowns | 3.92 | 2.83 | 2.41 | Assumed shipping and actual desired stock; not 50 confirmed tees. |
| Domestic $8 measured basket | 12.05 | 12.05 | 12.05 | $65 free-shipping policy; at 90% yield about $10.04. |
| Ontario sorter at $3/tee | N/A | 5.97 | 5.54 | An ask price, not an available carton. |

| EU €4 local stock, forward | 21.02 | 17.14 | 15.81 | Assumed intake and freight; not a quote. |

**Model:** landed = merchandise converted to CAD + origin shipping + proxy/packing + international or domestic delivery + duty + tax + clearance + FX/payment buffer. Divide by quantity × usable fraction. Usable means **meets fit, quality and style requirements**, not just wearable. No salvage revenue assumed. Inputs use [Bank of Canada Nov. 24 rates](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) (latest observed): USD 1.4296, JPY 0.008980, EUR 1.5679, GBP 1.8744 CAD; the model adds 2.5% to non-CAD payment costs as a planning buffer. Shipment weight is 220 g per tee plus packaging unless actual lot weight is known. Actual FX/duty conversion, dimensional billing, per-box fees, local sales/VAT, insurance and remote surcharges can differ. Check these at checkout.

**Import rules:** [CBSA](https://cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/services/cusma-aceum/lvs-efv-eng.html) lists a **$20** mail threshold and **$20** for most other-country courier shipments. Qualifying courier imports from the US/Mexico can be duty/tax free through **$40**, duty free but taxable through **$150**, and liable to both above $150. Eligibility is not guaranteed just because a US forwarding address is used; review [CBSA's exceptions and courier rules](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/publications/dm-md/d8/d8-2-16-eng.html). Do not split or misdeclare a single purchase to seek thresholds. Shipment country is not manufacturing origin.

The current [Customs Tariff](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/) has **18% MFN** for [6109 cotton and other knitted tees](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/html/02/ch61-eng.html); [6309.00.90 worn clothing](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/html/02/ch63-eng.html) also lists 18% MFN, and the [chapter note](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/01-99/ch63-2026-eng.pdf) requires appreciable wear and bulk packing for that heading. Do not declare wearable retail tees as duty-free wiping-rag feedstock. Request broker classification and origin evidence before assuming a preferential rate. The model uses [13% Ontario HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) for the personal cash-outlay benchmark and [Canada Post's $9.95 assessed-parcel handling fee](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/support/kb/receiving/delivery-faq/how-to-pay-duty-and-taxes-online-canada-post.page) where applicable. **Resale is commercial:** ask a broker about CARM/BN-RM, GST/federal component, provincial self-assessment and eligible input credits rather than using a personal exemption or assuming import HST matches this table. See [CBSA CARM](https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/services/carm/register-modify-account.html) and [CRA imports](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-imports-exports.html).

**Best international strategy:** first compare one US supplier's direct UPS destination quote with one US consolidation/courier quote; avoid multiple small intake parcels. [Shippsy](https://help.shippsy.com/article/whats-the-shippsy-fee-for) publishes handling from $9.99 excluding other charges; confirm commercial acceptance and Ottawa pickup/mailout. [MyUSAddress](https://myusaddress.ca/ot/) lists an Ogdensburg option and $29 plus S&H forwarding; for pickup include gas, bridge tolls, time and compliant border declaration. [Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/pricing)/[MyUS](https://www.myus.com/where-we-ship/canada/) are quote comparisons, not assumed discounts.

For measurement-led Japanese inventory, buy **one supported same-seller lot or one measured chain basket** and consolidate to one shipment, then decide between surface and air. [Neokyo](https://neokyo.com/en/fees) publishes ¥350 per request with packing from ¥500; [ZenMarket](https://zenmarket.jp/fees.aspx) lists ¥300–800/item and initial consolidation included, with deposit fees. Compare total cost, not just the headline fee. Buyee's fee page was inaccessible; no fee assumed. [Japan Post's Canada availability table](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/oversea/information/overview_en.html) lists air and surface, not SAL; [Zone 3](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/charge/list-parcel/zone3_en.html) lists 5 kg surface ¥4,900 vs air ¥12,450. Check proxy support, final pack dimensions and insurance; expect surface to be slow ([Japan Post's indicative Vancouver example](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/date/parcel/kawasaki_en.html) about two months, not an Ottawa promise). For Europe, [forward2me](https://www.forward2me.com/help/consolidation-vs-combine-and-repack/) publishes a guide to per-package repacking fees; favour one local warehouse dispatch. EU/UK and east/southeast Asian bale consolidation still need actual Canada quotes.

## 5. Recommended pilot and integration order

1. **Establish the local baseline:** 5–10 separately selectable items from Ottawa or a single domestic store. At 75% useful, cap the basket at **$30–60 landed** for the $8 target, or **$45–90** for exceptionally good fits at the $12 target. Use one seller and only cross a free-shipping threshold with items that independently meet the criteria.
2. **Request two Ottawa/GTA quotes for 25 intact tees:** target **$110 all-in** for a fit-agnostic carton (about $5.87 usable at 75%, $8.80 at 50%); lower the cap if delivery isn't local. Require actual lot images, grade ranges and six randomly chosen measurement examples. Don't pay for cut wiping cloth.
3. **Quote, don't yet buy, the Raghouse lot:** if pricing a 71-piece equivalent, cap at around **$280 all-in** to stay under $8 at 50% yield. At 25% that is nearly $16; reject it if the photos suggest unusable print/condition or a narrow fit window. Compare same gross weight, origin service and number of cartons.
4. **Test Japan only when measurement buys are intrinsically valuable:** first try one quote for ≥5 tees from one compatible seller/checkout, or one mixed auction with measurements. Use surface if allowed and slow arrival is acceptable. Reject if modeled effective cost exceeds $12 for generic tees; reserve $12–20 for unusually strong pieces. Don't buy 50 to prove a shipping spreadsheet.
5. **Build from authorized data only:** local CSV intake → one domestic merchant export (ItsADeal/People's Thrift) → approved eBay control → Detauri if export rights and Canada route confirmed → Raghouse lot/auction alerts and local grader manifests. Scraping Poshmark, Mercari or Raghouse is not a fallback.

For each pilot record delivered CAD, garment count, measurement coverage, width/length error against a tape, defects, and usable yield split by size, condition and style. Stop or change source after a trial misses the yield threshold; renegotiate better samples rather than buying more of the same grade. Ask a sorter for an exact first-carton spec: cleanable jersey tees, short sleeve, target width **20.5–21.25″**, length **26.5–27.25″** for fit-specific pulls, actual pieces in images, no cut/large holes, and separation of material or age confidence from marketing category.

## Scoring and limitations

Scores are evidence-weighted judgments, not audited KPIs: cost 25, measurement availability 20, selection 15, quality/vintage potential 15, automation 10, volume/replenishment 5, shipping 5, reliability 5. Each component is 0–10; contributions are weight × rating ÷ 10. Opportunity (1–10) is separate and can be high for an opaque pre-sort stream. Use evidence level before score; shipping uncertainty and absent data are not treated as confirmed positives. Dispatch frequency, exact counts, current MOQ/return rules and integration rights were not always public. The next highest-value research is to verify **five delivered quotes**, **three merchant permission responses** and **a random carton yield**, not to lengthen the directory.
